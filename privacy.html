PageReflow privacy policy

Applies to PageReflow for Windows, including the build distributed through the Microsoft Store. Last updated 26 November 2026.

PageReflow does not collect, transmit or sell any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network access of any kind. Everything it does happens on your own computer.

What happens to the files you convert

  • PageReflow opens only the PDFs and folders you choose or drop on the window.
  • It reads those files and writes HTML files, and images inlined inside them, to the output folder you pick. Nothing is copied anywhere else.
  • The application makes no network connections, so no document, file name or path can leave your machine through it.

What is stored on your computer

  • Your preferences - conversion mode, image resolution, output folder, and the overwrite and open-when-done switches - are saved to %APPDATA%\PageReflow\settings.json so the app opens the way you left it. That file stays on your computer and is not sent anywhere. Deleting it is safe; the app falls back to its defaults.
  • The HTML pages PageReflow produces can remember tick marks for interactive checklists. Those marks are kept in your web browser's local storage for that page, on your machine. Nothing about them is transmitted.
  • Uninstalling PageReflow from Windows Settings removes the app. It does not remove the settings file or any pages you converted.

What is not collected

  • No personal information, contact details, location or device identifiers.
  • No usage statistics, crash reports or diagnostic data.
  • No cookies and no tracking of any kind, in the app or in the pages it writes.
  • No third-party services, SDKs or content delivery networks.

Children

The app is a document converter. It is suitable for general audiences and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address and its date updated. Because the app has no way to contact you, the page here is the whole of the notice.
